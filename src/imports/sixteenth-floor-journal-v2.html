Learnings

What this journey teaches us

Clear priorities
01

Markets don't wait

Their ₹1 Cr budget seemed realistic initially. Within two years, prices rose to ₹1.35–1.55 Cr. Starting earlier would have offered more flexibility and choice.

02

Shortlists beat browsing

After comparing many listings and projects, they found more options didn't help. Focusing on a few well-matched communities made decisions easier.

03

Builder trust matters

Price wasn't the only factor. Quality, clear documentation, RERA compliance and delivery history mattered as much as the floor plan.

04

Buy for tomorrow, not today

Their priorities shifted after learning they'd become parents. Schools, hospitals, safety and convenience became more valuable than luxury.
